## Selection heuristics

- **Risk / irreversibility** → premortem, margin of safety, expected value, optionality
- **Stuck / no options visible** → first principles, framing, inversion
- **Continue or quit** → sunk cost, opportunity cost, regret minimization
- **Is there demand / validating an idea** → the Mom Test, sampling, confirmation bias
- **Money or position sizing** → expected value & Kelly, margin of safety, randomness
- **Too much to do** → Pareto, bottlenecks, opportunity cost
- **People / team behaviour** → incentives, social proof, Hanlon's razor
- **Judging results or evidence** → regression to the mean, randomness, sampling
- **Change that won't start or won't stop** → activation energy, inertia, feedback loops
- **Career / venture portfolio** → barbell, optionality, compounding, circle of competence
